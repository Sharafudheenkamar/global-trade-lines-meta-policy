User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Global Trade Lines may use Meta APIs to display or manage content from authorized Facebook Pages and Instagram Professional accounts.

How to Request Deletion

If you have authorized our application and want applicable information associated with your account or authorization deleted, contact Global Trade Lines through the official contact details published on our business website and use the subject Meta Data Deletion Request.

What to Include

Include enough non-sensitive information for us to identify the relevant authorization, such as your Facebook Page name or Instagram Professional account name. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or other credentials.

What Happens Next

After the request is verified, we will delete applicable information stored by our application where appropriate, subject to security, legal, regulatory, or legitimate record-keeping requirements.

Removing App Access

You may also remove access granted to connected applications through your Facebook or Instagram account settings. Removing authorization may prevent our application from accessing future data from your account.

Questions

For questions regarding deletion or privacy, contact Global Trade Lines through the official contact information published on our business website.
