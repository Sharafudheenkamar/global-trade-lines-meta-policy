Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

1. Overview

Global Trade Lines respects user privacy and is committed to handling information responsibly. This policy explains how information may be accessed and used when our website or connected services use Meta products such as Facebook and Instagram.

2. Information We May Access

Our website may use Meta APIs to access information from Facebook Pages and Instagram Professional accounts authorized for use with our application. Depending on permissions granted, this may include posts, captions, images, videos, media URLs, timestamps, page or account identifiers, and related metadata.

3. How We Use Information

Information obtained through Meta APIs is used to display and manage authorized Facebook and Instagram content on the Global Trade Lines website, including social media posts, images, videos, reels, and related gallery content.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information obtained through our Meta integration. Information may be processed by service providers where necessary to operate, secure, host, or maintain our website and related services.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information and credentials used by our website. Access tokens, app secrets, and similar credentials are kept server-side and are not intentionally exposed in public website code.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to operational, security, and legal requirements.

7. Third-Party Services

Our website may interact with services provided by Meta Platforms, Inc., including Facebook and Instagram. Use of those services is also subject to Meta's applicable terms, policies, and privacy practices.

8. Data Deletion

If you have authorized our application and want applicable information associated with your authorization deleted, please follow our User Data Deletion Instructions.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please contact Global Trade Lines through the official contact information published on our business website.
